How to Play Chameleon Protocol
1. The Secret Briefing

All Agents see the secret word (e.g. "Taco Bell"). The Chameleon only sees the category and has NO IDEA what the word is!

2. The 1-Word Clues

Taking turns, every player gives a 1-word clue. Don't be too obvious or the Chameleon will guess the word. Don't be too vague or you'll look like the Chameleon!

3. Voting & The Steal

Vote on who looks suspicious. If the Chameleon is caught, they get ONE last chance to guess the secret word to steal the win!
