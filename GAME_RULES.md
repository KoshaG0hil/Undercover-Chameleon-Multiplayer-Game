# How to Play: Undercover Chameleon Protocol

A rapid social deduction and bluffing party game where everyone knows the secret word except one disguised impostor.

---

## 🎯 The Objective

- **Agents:** Identify and vote out the Chameleon before they deduce the secret word.
- **The Chameleon:** Blend in seamlessly during the clue phase, avoid detection during the vote, or steal the win by guessing the secret word!

---

## 🕹️ Game Phases

### 1. The Confidential Briefing (8s)
- All players view their role.
- **Agents** see the Category and the Secret Word (e.g., `FOOD: PEPPERONI PIZZA`).
- **The Chameleon** sees only the Category and `??? - YOU ARE THE CHAMELEON!`.

### 2. Clue Transmissions (30s per turn)
- Taking turns, each player types a **single 1-word clue** (e.g. *"Cheesy"*, *"Crust"*, *"Italian"*).
- **Agent Strategy:** Don't be too obvious (or the Chameleon will guess the word), but don't be too vague (or you'll look like the Chameleon!).
- **Chameleon Strategy:** Listen carefully to previous clues, give a plausible word that fits the category, and stay cool!

### 3. Emergency Suspect Voting (45s)
- Once all clues are transmitted, all players review the clues and vote for who they suspect is the Chameleon.
- If the vote is tied or inconclusive, the Chameleon escapes into the shadows!

### 4. Chameleon's Last Stand
- If the Chameleon is correctly caught, they get **one final chance to steal the victory** by selecting the secret word from a list of candidates!

---

## 🏆 Scoring System

| Outcome | Winner | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Chameleon avoids detection | **Chameleon** | Chameleon +2 Points |
| Chameleon is caught, but guesses secret word | **Chameleon** | Chameleon +2 Points |
| Chameleon is caught and fails guess | **Agents** | Each Agent +1 Point |
